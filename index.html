COMMON QUESTIONS

Audio to Text FAQ

Is this audio to text converter free?

There is a limited free allowance. Availability depends on current daily and overall service limits, which protect the shared transcription budget.

Which audio formats can I upload?

Supported completed recordings include MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, OGG, MP4 and some WebM files. Files must pass format, audio-track, size and duration checks. Incomplete browser recordings may be rejected.

How long can a recording be?

Each file can contain up to 15 minutes of verified audio and must be no larger than 24 MB.

Can the tool transcribe Chinese or mixed-language speech?

Automatic language detection can process multiple languages, including Chinese and English. Results depend on recording quality; always verify important details.

Can I edit and save the transcript?

Yes. Edit the transcript in the text box, copy it, or export it as TXT or a Word-compatible DOCX document.

Are my uploaded files saved?

The server processes temporary uploaded files for transcription. You should avoid submitting sensitive recordings that you are not authorized to share. Your edited transcript remains in this browser tab until you close or reload it.
