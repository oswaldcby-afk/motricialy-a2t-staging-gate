FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this audio to text converter free?

There is a limited free allowance. Availability depends on current daily and overall service limits, which protect the shared transcription budget.

Which audio formats can I upload or record?

Upload completed MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, OGG, MP4 or supported WebM audio. For compatible MP4, MOV, MKV and WebM videos, use on-device audio extraction first. Browser recording creates a WAV; codec support varies.

How long can a recording be?

Each file can contain up to 15 minutes of verified audio and must be no larger than 24 MB.

Can the tool transcribe Chinese or mixed-language speech?

Yes. Choose Mandarin, English, or English + Chinese when those languages are expected. Automatic detection is also available. Always check names, numbers and code-switched speech.

Can I edit, summarize and download a transcript?

Yes. Edit the original and AI-cleaned versions separately, create optional summaries, or manually time subtitle cues. Download TXT, DOCX, Markdown, SRT or VTT. AI text operations have a separate daily limit.

What happens to my audio and transcript?

Audio is uploaded and sent to OpenAI when you choose Transcribe. Optional AI cleanup or summary sends your selected transcript text to OpenAI separately. The site handles uploads as temporary processing files; editable results remain in the current tab unless you download them. Avoid submitting recordings you are not authorized to share.
