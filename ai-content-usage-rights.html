Is AI-generated content protected by copyright?

Not necessarily. Copyright protection depends on the law of the relevant country and the amount and nature of human creative contribution. Some entirely AI-generated material may not qualify for copyright protection. Other people may also receive similar AI output. You should not assume that a generated result is unique or that you can prevent others from creating something similar.

Special rules for Text to Speech

Audio generated with Motricialy Text to Speech may be used in eligible personal and commercial projects, including video narration and podcast voiceovers. You retain your rights in the script you submit, and the generated audio is subject to the AI Output permission described above.

  • Disclose AI speech: Clearly communicate to your audience that the voice is AI-generated, rather than a human speaker. Additional rules may apply on the platform where you publish.
  • Do not claim ownership of a voice: Generating audio using Marin, Ash, Alloy, or another built-in voice does not transfer ownership of that synthetic voice, its underlying model, or the prerecorded voice previews.
  • Do not impersonate someone without rights: Do not falsely suggest that a real person spoke, endorsed, or approved your content. Permission may be required to reproduce or imitate someone's identity or voice.
  • Review your recording: AI narration can mispronounce names, omit words, or make other errors. Listen before publishing important or commercial material.

How these rules apply to other AI tools

The same general principles apply across Motricialy AI features, but the underlying rights differ by task:

  • AI Humanizer or rewriting: Rewriting someone else's text does not remove the original author's copyright or other applicable restrictions.
  • AI Logo Generator or image generation: Commercial permission does not ensure that a logo or image is original, free of third-party rights, or eligible for trademark registration or exclusive use.
  • AI PDF Summarizer: You need appropriate rights to upload or process a PDF. A generated summary does not grant rights to redistribute the source document.
  • Audio to Text: A transcript does not change the rights in the original recording, the speaker's voice, or the spoken content.
